# Kleihaus Backlink And Partnership Outreach Plan

Date: 2026-07-08

This plan documents ethical backlink and partnership opportunities. Do not create fake backlinks, fake partnerships, fake supplier relationships, fake reviews or paid links that violate platform rules.

```text
[URL]

We only want the listing included if it accurately reflects the real relationship and helps customers find the right finishing-material support.

Thank you,
Kleihaus Ceramics
```

## Tracking

Maintain a simple outreach log:

Track monthly:

- New referring domains.
- New backlinks by destination page.
- Referral traffic.
- Leads or quote requests from referral traffic.
- Listings that need NAP corrections.

## Guardrails

- Do not buy spam links.
- Do not create fake partner pages.
- Do not claim supplier relationships that are not real.
- Do not publish fake case studies.
- Do not use fake review/rating markup.
- Prefer fewer relevant links over many low-quality listings.
