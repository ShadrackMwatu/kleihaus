## Suggested Page Structure

### Project Title

Use a clear title such as:

```text
Bathroom tile and sanitaryware support for a Nairobi apartment renovation
```

Only name the location and project type if accurate and approved.

### Location

State the general area, for example Nairobi, Machakos or Makueni. Avoid publishing a private address unless the customer explicitly approves it.

### Project Type

Examples:

- Apartment renovation
- New home finishing
- Retail fit-out
- Bathroom upgrade
- Rental unit refresh
- Commercial flooring

### Customer Need

Explain the real problem:

- Needed durable floor tiles for a high-traffic area.
- Needed coordinated bathroom tiles, taps and shower fittings.
- Needed paint guidance for interior and exterior surfaces.
- Needed adhesive, grout and installation material support.
- Needed delivery planning for a project site.

### Products Or Material Categories Used

Use categories, not unsupported product offers:

- Floor tiles
- Wall tiles
- Bathroom tiles
- Sanitaryware
- Taps and mixers
- Paints
- Adhesives and grout
- Trims, tools or accessories

Do not add fake SKU, price, availability or Offer details.

### Support Provided By Kleihaus

Describe real support:

- Quantity planning
- Product matching
- Finish coordination
- Adhesive/grout guidance
- Delivery coordination
- Installation workflow guidance
- Fundi or project team technical support

### Delivery Or Installation Guidance

Document practical details:

- Site access considerations
- Delivery timing constraints
- Surface preparation notes
- Cutting/drilling/fixing considerations
- Grouting, cleaning or handover guidance

### Outcome

Describe the real outcome without exaggeration:

- Materials were coordinated for the room.
- The customer received a clearer quote plan.
- Delivery was planned around site access.
- Installation materials were matched to the tile and surface.

Avoid fake ratings, fake review quotes or unsupported performance claims.

- Customer/project permission confirmed.
