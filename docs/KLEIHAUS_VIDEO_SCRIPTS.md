## On-screen copy rules

Keep each frame to one idea and no more than two short lines where possible. Use “supplied or supported by Kleihaus” for project imagery until project involvement is confirmed. Use “guidance” and “planning” for support language. Do not add “best”, “guaranteed”, “available now”, price claims, discounts, named brands, reviews or project results without approval.

## Audio direction

Use a warm, natural professional voice with measured East African English pronunciation. Record a human voice or use a high-quality licensed service after approval. Select restrained instrumental music with light percussion, warm keys or soft marimba-like texture only when properly licensed. Do not download or publish copyrighted commercial music without written permission. A voice-only version is acceptable.
