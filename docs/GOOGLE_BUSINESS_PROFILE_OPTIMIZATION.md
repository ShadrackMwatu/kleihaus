## Post Ideas

Use a weekly or monthly rhythm. Posts should be short, useful and connected to real services.

- Tile buying tip: room use, slip resistance and quantity planning.
- Bathroom renovation planning: coordinate tiles, taps, showers and accessories before buying.
- Paint selection tip: match paint to surface exposure and preparation needs.
- Adhesive and grout reminder: fixing materials affect the final tile finish.
- Installation support note: share measurements and photos before requesting guidance.
- Location-focused support: Nairobi apartment projects, Machakos build planning, Makueni logistics-aware orders.
- Seasonal renovation reminder: plan delivery and site readiness before materials are needed.
- New showroom photos: highlight genuine available displays without fake prices or unsupported availability claims.

## Review Request Workflow

Ask for genuine reviews only after a real customer interaction.

Suggested workflow:

1. Confirm the customer received support, materials or quote assistance.
2. Ask if they would be comfortable sharing an honest Google review.
3. Send the official Google review link manually.
4. Thank them regardless of rating.
5. Respond professionally to reviews without exposing private order details.

Do not:

- Offer discounts, gifts or incentives for reviews.
- Ask staff, friends or vendors to create fake reviews.
- Write reviews on behalf of customers.
- Add fake ratings, reviews or AggregateRating schema to the website.
