# Kleihaus business content requirements

These items require Kleihaus confirmation or supplied material. They should not be inferred from design, imagery or analytics.

| Priority | Required material | Why it matters | Minimum evidence |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| P1 | Physical location/showroom details and opening hours | Removes the largest local trust gap | Confirmed public wording and location pin |
| P1 | Delivery areas, constraints and planning expectations | Sets accurate purchase expectations | Approved service-area list and qualification rules |
| P1 | Product catalogue details | Enables useful product discovery | Names, dimensions, finishes, uses, pack/quantity and compatibility where applicable |
| P1 | Warranty, returns and payment guidance | Reduces enquiry hesitation | Approved policy wording |
| P1 | Completed-project photographs with context | Converts visual interest into proof | Permission, Kleihaus involvement, broad project type and materials supplied/support provided |
| P2 | Verified customer testimonials | Supports trust without generic claims | Customer consent, name or approved attribution and exact wording |
| P2 | Supplier/brand relationships | Strengthens product credibility | Current authorization or approved list |
| P2 | Showroom, warehouse or delivery photographs | Makes the operating business visible | Current images with permission and no sensitive metadata |
| P2 | Team or specialist profiles | Makes expertise concrete | Names, roles and approved biographies |
| P3 | Maintenance and care guidance | Adds post-purchase usefulness | Product-specific instructions approved by the supplier |

Do not publish a claim until its source and approval are recorded. Avoid customer names, precise addresses, project dates, prices, inventory, certifications and results unless explicitly confirmed.
