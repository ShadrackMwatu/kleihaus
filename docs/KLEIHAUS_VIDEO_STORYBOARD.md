## End-card and accessibility notes

Hold the final card long enough to read on mobile. Include captions in all social exports. Export a captionless master only as a production source, not as the primary social version. Avoid placing important copy over faces, fixtures or tile detail. Provide a descriptive post caption and alt text for each uploaded video.
