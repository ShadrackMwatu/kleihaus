# Local SEO And Google Business Profile Checklist

This checklist is for manual follow-up outside the repository. Do not store Google Business Profile credentials, Search Console credentials or private business verification material in the repo.

## Google Business Profile

- Confirm the primary category matches the business accurately, such as building materials supplier, tile store, home improvement store or ceramics supplier depending on the available GBP category options.
- Add services that match the website copy: tiles, sanitaryware, paints, adhesives, grout, tools, finishing advisory, delivery coordination, installation support and product/application training.
- Add product/service entries for core categories without fake prices if prices vary by brand, quantity and availability.
- Keep NAP details consistent with the website:
  - Name: Kleihaus Ceramics
  - Phone/WhatsApp: +254 748 827 166
  - Email: sales@kleihaus.com
  - Website: https://www.kleihaus.com/
  - Service areas: Nairobi, Machakos, Makueni and wider Kenya where practical
- Upload real photos of tiles, sanitaryware, paints, adhesives, tools, showroom/project displays and completed installation support where permitted.
- Add GBP Q&A answers for common customer questions: tile prices, sanitaryware prices, paint prices, delivery, installation support and how to request a quote.
- Ask real customers for reviews after completed service. Do not create fake reviews or incentivize misleading reviews.

## Website And Campaign Tracking

- Use UTM tracking for GBP website links if desired, for example:
  `https://www.kleihaus.com/?utm_source=google_business_profile&utm_medium=organic&utm_campaign=local_search`
- Keep phone, email and service-area details aligned between GBP, website footer, structured data and social profiles.
- Use the same canonical domain consistently: `https://www.kleihaus.com/`.

## Search Console Follow-Up

- Submit `https://www.kleihaus.com/sitemap.xml` after deployment.
- Inspect important URLs after deployment:
  - `/`
  - `/tiles`
  - `/locations/nairobi`
  - `/locations/machakos`
  - `/locations/makueni`
  - `/tiles-nairobi`
  - `/sanitaryware-nairobi`
  - `/paints-nairobi`
  - `/installation-support-nairobi`
  - `/tile-buying-guide`
  - `/cost-estimation-guide`
- Monitor indexing, crawl errors, canonical selection, mobile usability and enhancement warnings.
- Track keyword impressions for local queries around tiles, sanitaryware, paints, adhesives, grout, delivery and installation support.
- Use the Rich Results Test for the homepage and several route-aware pages to confirm no Product/Offer/AggregateRating/review schema warnings appear.

## Content Maintenance

- Keep local landing pages accurate. Use service-area language unless a physical branch is verified for a location.
- Add real project photos, FAQs and location details only when they are true and maintainable.
- Keep pricing language quote-led unless current product-level prices, availability and offer details are displayed on the site.
