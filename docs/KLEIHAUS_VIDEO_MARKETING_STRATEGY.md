# Kleihaus video marketing strategy

## Campaign

Campaign line: **Beautiful spaces begin with the right finishes.**

Brand: **Kleihaus Ceramics**

Positioning: Tiles, sanitaryware, paints and related finishing materials with product selection, quantity, delivery and installation guidance for homes, renovations and commercial or project enquiries.

The campaign uses real Kleihaus repository imagery, calm architectural movement and one clear action: explore products or contact Kleihaus at `kleihaus.com`. It does not imply that Kleihaus installed every pictured space.

## Format plan

| Version | Format | Primary use | Treatment |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| A | 15 seconds, 1080x1920 | Instagram Reels, Facebook Reels, WhatsApp Status | Fast category introduction, five to six stills, one CTA |
| B | 30 seconds, 1080x1920 | Reels, Stories, paid social after approval | More product context and support message |
| C | 45-60 seconds, 1920x1080 | Website, YouTube, presentations and social | Premium brand film with slower editorial pacing |

## Visual rules

Use 0.5-1.0 second crossfades, restrained 3-5% scale movement, straight cuts only when a product category changes, and large high-contrast type with generous margins. Keep text inside a 9:16 safe zone: 120px from each side and 220px from top/bottom. For 16:9, keep text 8% inside each edge. Use Kleihaus green as an accent, white text over darkened images and a clean neutral end card.

Do not use stock footage, AI footage, fake customer scenes, excessive zooms, spinning logos, template effects, loud transitions or autoplay video on the homepage. Videos remain standalone assets until separately approved for website integration.

## Distribution and measurement

Publish organic versions to Instagram, Facebook, WhatsApp Status, YouTube and approved presentations. Use a UTM-tagged website CTA when the platform allows it, for example `utm_source=instagram&utm_medium=organic_social&utm_campaign=kleihaus_video_15s`. Track aggregate landing-page and contact events; do not put names, phone numbers, message text or customer records in campaign parameters.
