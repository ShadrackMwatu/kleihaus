# Kleihaus competitive action plan

## 0-30 days

- Connect read-only Search Console and GA4 reporting; establish query, landing-page and contact-event baselines.
- Add verified showroom, hours, delivery and response information.
- Audit overlap between `/tiles`, `/tiles-kenya`, `/tiles-nairobi` and related service-location pages using actual queries before changing URLs.
- Confirm product facts for tiles, sanitaryware, sinks/mixers, paints, adhesive, grout and tools.
- Give the `/trade-projects` page an owner-approved enquiry checklist for the nine segments in the target-client map.
- Keep CTAs restrained and measure quote, WhatsApp, phone and email outcomes privately.

## 31-90 days

- Publish one substantive tile-quantity or adhesive-planning tool after technical assumptions are approved.
- Add a specification-led sanitaryware and kitchen-sink buying guide.
- Publish two or three approved project case studies with materials and Kleihaus involvement clearly stated.
- Add an approved delivery-planning page only when service areas and terms are confirmed.
- Build relationships with contractors, designers, developers and relevant local directories; record referrals with consent.

## 3-6 months

- Introduce catalogue filtering or comparison if product data and search behaviour justify it.
- Add trade reorder and project-phase follow-up processes.
- Expand local targeting only where delivery evidence and unique local value exist.
- Compare organic acquisition by segment, product, location, channel and outcome.

## 6-12 months

- Develop a private acquisition and retention dashboard with Search Console, GA4 and owner outcome data.
- Build a library of approved technical guides, case studies and maintenance content.
- Evaluate lightweight CRM integration only if lead volume and follow-up leakage justify it.

Do not publish mass-generated location pages, fake proof, unsupported catalogue facts or competitor-style prices/ratings without verified data.
