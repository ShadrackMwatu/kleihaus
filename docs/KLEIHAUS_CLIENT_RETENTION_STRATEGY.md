# Kleihaus client retention strategy

This is a consent-based operational plan. It does not authorize unsolicited WhatsApp or email messaging and does not require a CRM implementation before the business process is proven.

## Lifecycle gaps

The website supports discovery, consideration, enquiry and quote preparation. Delivery, installation, post-purchase care, reorder reminders, referral capture and won/lost outcomes require business-side records. GA4 should measure aggregate actions; it should not hold identities, message text or order details.

## Segment playbook

| Segment | Useful follow-up | Repeat-business opportunity | Owner evidence/process |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Homeowners | Send approved care/maintenance guidance after purchase | Complementary grout, trims, accessories, paint touch-ups and future rooms | Consent and purchase reference |
| Contractors/fundis | Keep approved technical sheets and reorder list available | Repeat tile, adhesive, grout and tool orders across jobs | Trade account or enquiry register |
| Developers | Review project phases and outstanding quantities | Phased procurement, repeat units and maintenance cycles | Project status and quote register |
| Designers/architects | Provide approved samples/specification references | Repeat specification and client-project referrals | Approved contact preference and project ID |
| Hardware/resellers | Share current approved range and trade updates | Reorders and complementary categories | Trade terms and consented updates |
| Commercial clients | Schedule useful project-stage check-ins | Maintenance, refurbishments and new sites | Account owner and service calendar |
| Hospitality/institutional | Provide care, replacement and phase guidance | Room blocks, facilities maintenance and later projects | Procurement contact and approved cadence |

## Minimal private outcome register

Record a random lead reference, date, landing source if known, channel, broad location, product group, status `NEW`, `QUALIFIED`, `QUOTED`, `WON` or `LOST`, next action date and optional order value/currency. Record `UNKNOWN` when the source is not known. Keep the register access-controlled and separate from the public dashboard and GA4.

## Five retention priorities

1. Assign an owner to every qualified enquiry and record the next action.
2. Confirm delivery and installation guidance after a quote is accepted.
3. Send useful, consented care and maintenance information.
4. Ask trade/project customers whether future phases or complementary categories are planned.
5. Request referrals or testimonials only after a satisfactory, verified interaction and with permission.

Measure repeat enquiries, repeat orders, time to next enquiry, quote-to-won rate and referral source only after a baseline exists. Do not use raw WhatsApp clicks as repeat customers.
