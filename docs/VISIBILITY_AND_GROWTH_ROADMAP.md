# Kleihaus Visibility and Growth Roadmap

This roadmap separates public customer-facing visibility work from backend management intelligence. The website should remain a clean ceramics catalogue, while analytics, reporting and AI recommendations stay in service layers or future backend infrastructure.

## Google Business Profile Setup Checklist

- Claim or create the official Kleihaus Ceramics profile.
- Use the verified business name: Kleihaus Ceramics.
- Add phone: +254 748 827 166.
- Add email: sales@kleihaus.com.
- Add website: https://www.kleihaus.com/.
- Add service areas: Nairobi, Machakos and Makueni.
- Add categories related to tiles, sanitaryware, paints and building finishing materials.
- Upload verified Kleihaus photos only.
- Add opening hours after confirmation.
- Ask real customers for reviews after completed purchases or projects.

## Google Search Console Checklist

- Verify https://www.kleihaus.com/.
- Submit sitemap: https://www.kleihaus.com/sitemap.xml.
- Monitor indexing for homepage and future landing pages.
- Track search terms for tiles, bathroom tiles, sanitaryware, grout, adhesives and paints in Kenya.
- Watch Core Web Vitals and mobile usability.
- Use discovered search queries to prioritize helpful guide topics.

## Google Analytics 4 Checklist

- Configure `VITE_GA_MEASUREMENT_ID=` only when the official GA4 property is ready.
- Keep `VITE_ENABLE_ANALYTICS=false` until consent and measurement approach are confirmed.
- Track customer-facing lead events only:
  - `search_submitted`
  - `category_clicked`
  - `product_interest_clicked`
  - `whatsapp_cta_clicked`
  - `quote_form_submitted`
  - `guide_topic_clicked`
- Avoid sending names, phone numbers, email addresses or free-form quote messages into behavioral analytics.

## SEO Landing Page Plan

Phase 1 keeps SEO-ready sections inside the single-page app for:

- Tiles in Nairobi
- Bathroom Tiles Kenya
- Sanitaryware Kenya
- Tile Adhesive and Grout Kenya
- Paints and Finishes Kenya
- Modern Bathroom Designs Kenya
- 60x60 Tiles Kenya

Future expansion can turn these sections into dedicated pages or routes with original Kleihaus copy, product images, FAQs, quote CTAs and internal links.

## Social Media Content Plan

- Post real Kleihaus product displays, installations, delivery updates and customer education.
- Use short WhatsApp-first captions for quote requests.
- Avoid fake social links on the website until official profiles are verified.
- Suggested content themes:
  - Bathroom tile combinations
  - 60x60 floor tile ideas
  - Adhesive and grout tips
  - Paint colour guidance
  - Sanitaryware selection tips
  - Before-requesting-a-quote measurement tips

## Monthly AI Report Plan

Monthly reports should be generated by backend/service-layer infrastructure and sent later through a secure endpoint to:

- muthamimwatu@gmail.com
- sales@kleihaus.com

Reports should include:

- Top searches
- Most clicked categories
- WhatsApp inquiry trends
- Quote request trends
- Guide topics clicked
- Emerging product interests
- Recommended SEO topics
- Recommended inventory focus

Do not expose monthly intelligence dashboards on the public website.

## WhatsApp Lead Conversion Plan

- Keep WhatsApp visible in the header, product cards, category cards, contact section and mobile sticky CTA.
- Prefill WhatsApp messages with product or category context.
- Keep the quote form submitting to `/api/quote-request`.
- Keep manual WhatsApp links available as customer fallback.
- Use `VITE_MONTHLY_REPORT_ENDPOINT=` later for secure monthly reporting delivery.
- Never place WhatsApp tokens, email API keys, SMTP passwords or database credentials in frontend code.
