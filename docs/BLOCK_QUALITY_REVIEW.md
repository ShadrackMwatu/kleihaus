- Eleven Node SEO regression tests and eight static analytics mappings pass.

- No quote submitted to the real backend. Live email delivery, actual phone/email/WhatsApp handoff and GA4 collection are not proved by mocked/static checks. No absolute guarantee of zero defects or exhaustive manual review of every route is claimed.
