# 30-day organic client-acquisition program

Start the measurement window when read-only access is approved. Baselines are currently unavailable, not zero. No rankings, sales or percentage increases are promised.

## Days 1-3: evidence and baseline

Owner: confirm GA4/Search Console properties, authorize reporting access and assign a business outcome owner. Engineering: resolve the WhatsApp verification failure, capture remaining events, verify the active Workers deployment and actual scheduled monitor runs. Export the previous complete 28 days and record timezone, filters, extraction date and comparison window.

First KPIs: baseline established for impressions, clicks, CTR, position, indexed pages, organic users/sessions, channel-specific contact clicks, quote submissions, qualified leads, won customers and attributable revenue. Where unavailable, record pending and named owner. Separate test traffic using its existing playwright/e2e campaign; do not count it as acquisition.

## Days 4-7: commercial relevance

Compare Search Console queries and landing pages for tiles Kenya/Nairobi/Machakos/Makueni, sanitaryware and adhesive searches. Review competing national/category pages before changing canonicals. Validate sink category copy against actual imagery. Confirm service coverage and product specifications. Review paths from product to guide/project/contact without adding repeated CTAs.

## Week 2: useful content

Prioritize the scored content backlog: tile quantities, adhesive quantities, bathroom planning and sanitaryware buying. Expand the existing cost, installation and paint guides before introducing competing pages. Calculator publication requires tested units, manufacturer-specific coverage assumptions and clear estimate limits. Warehouse recommendations need actual suitability evidence. Publish only substantive owner-reviewed material.

## Week 3: local trust and GBP

Use existing GBP_SOCIAL_DRAFTS.md: week 1 tile planning, week 2 sanitaryware, week 3 paint education, week 4 project planning. Owner checks every fact/image before posting. Add approved delivery information and project context; no invented discounts or reviews. Track GBP links with utm_source=google&utm_medium=organic&utm_campaign=gbp and a stable content label per post. Record GBP separately from ordinary organic search in analysis.

## Week 4: outcome review

Compare complete equal-length periods and annotate deployments, posts and content changes. Review impressions/clicks by query and page, organic sessions by landing page, contact-intent events by channel, unique qualified leads and won outcomes in the private register. A session with two channel clicks is not automatically two leads. Report CTR as clicks/impressions and session lead-intent rate using sessions with at least one primary event, not raw event count divided by sessions. Use null for zero-denominator rates.

Set numeric growth targets only after observing a credible baseline and available sales capacity. Low volumes require longer observation; attribution does not establish causation. At day 30, report observed change and uncertainty, not guaranteed SEO impact.
